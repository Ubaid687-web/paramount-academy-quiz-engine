## Why analysis and activation are different

Material analysis records the textbook structure, papers, patterns, and MCQ design rules. Curriculum activation is the separate operational step that stores one versioned textbook transcript, reviewed chapter page boundaries, half-chapter segments, and reusable scopes for live selection in the teacher builder. The activation step does **not** mean the material was newly requested or newly supplied.
