# MCQ Pattern Evidence and Current-Year Paper Intake

## Current online-quiz scope

The live quiz product is **MCQ-only**. Short questions, long questions, paper composition, mark schemes, expected answers, rubrics, and manual-answer review are intentionally outside the online quiz flow at this stage.

The verified user-supplied textbook is the content boundary. The verified model and annual papers are **pattern evidence**, not a substitute for the textbook and not a source for invented questions.

## How supplied papers guide MCQ drafting

When the platform creates an MCQ draft, its selected textbook chapter or teacher-supplied custom source controls the concepts that may be assessed. Verified paper evidence is used only to tune the style of assessment toward three board-relevant categories:

| Category | Purpose in an online MCQ | Required grounding |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge and recall | Checks essential definitions, facts, symbols, terminology, and direct relationships from the selected source. | The selected textbook chapter or custom source must state the answer. |
| Conceptual understanding | Tests cause-and-effect, distinctions, interpretation, and relationships between source-grounded concepts. | The question and answer must be supported by the selected source. |
| Applied understanding | Uses a short, source-supported situation, calculation, or comparison to test use of a learned concept. | The required method, rule, and answer must be supported by the selected source. |

No question may be generated merely because it resembles an annual-paper question. The paper evidence informs difficulty balance and board style; the verified book or custom source supplies the examinable content.

## Intake procedure for the upcoming current-year paper

When the user supplies the new paper, it will be treated as new evidence rather than assumed to match previous years.

| Stage | Required action | Output | Safety rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Preserve | Store the exact user-supplied PDF and a page-preserving transcript. | Auditable file record and transcript. | Do not substitute a web copy or a guessed version. |
| 2. Extract | Identify the MCQ section, item count, compulsory/choice rules, marks, time, and subject-specific wording. | Paper-structure record. | Record only what the paper shows. |
| 3. Compare | Compare the new MCQ structure with the active supplied model and annual-paper evidence. | Change log: unchanged, added, removed, or revised pattern elements. | Do not alter generation rules until the comparison is reviewed. |
| 4. Approve | Require Portal Admin review of the extracted pattern change. | Active or pending pattern-source status. | The new pattern stays inactive until reviewed. |
| 5. Apply | Update the MCQ balance and pattern guidance for the relevant subject. | Versioned MCQ pattern rule. | Continue to use only verified textbook/custom source content for every question. |

## Current source boundary

Physics and Computer Science have active verified textbook paths. The stored Chemistry transcript is archived because the user confirmed it is not the supplied Chemistry textbook. Chemistry online MCQs can use a teacher-provided custom source only until the exact textbook is verified.
