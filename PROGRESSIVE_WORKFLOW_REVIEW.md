# Progressive Workflow Review

## Live review — 13 August 2026

The Assessment Draft Studio now opens with only **Step 1: Choose format** visible. The live page confirms that the source controls, question-count controls, difficulty settings, and draft-generation action remain hidden until the teacher chooses an assessment format and completes the source step.

The header provides a three-step progress rail: **Choose format → Select source → Configure & draft**. Its active state is visible, future steps remain unavailable until their prerequisite step is complete, and the saved-draft recovery form is collapsed behind an **Open saved draft** action.

The initial Grade 10 Physics screen preserves a compact context summary and a direct Change action while avoiding the previous all-at-once configuration layout. Source-safety requirements and assessment availability remain enforced by the existing backend contracts.
