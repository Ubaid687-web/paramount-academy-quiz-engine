## Guardrails

The Physics workflow currently has **one supplied model-paper source**, while Chemistry and Computer Science have multiple active supplied sources. The full-paper builder must display and require selection of the source or sources underpinning the draft.

For Chemistry, the model paper's four equal five-mark extended questions and the two past papers' visible 6+6+4+4 extended-question pattern are deliberately kept separate. The platform does not merge them into a fabricated universal distribution.

All source PDFs and their page-preserving OCR transcripts are stored in managed project storage. New documents must be preserved, extracted, registered, and reviewed as separate evidence before changing any assessment workflow.
