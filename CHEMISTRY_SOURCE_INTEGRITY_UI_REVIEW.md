# Chemistry Source-Integrity and Teacher UI Review

## Live verification — 13 August 2026

The live Grade 10 Chemistry builder confirms that the archived Chemistry transcript is no longer presented as an approved textbook. The format screen marks MCQ as **Custom source only** and leaves short questions, long questions, and full-paper drafting unavailable until a verified Chemistry textbook is active.

After selecting MCQ, the source step exposes only **My custom lesson material**. The page states: **No verified Chemistry textbook is active. Use a custom source.** There is no approved-chapter selector, chapter list, or archived textbook label available to the teacher.

The teacher interface was also reduced to operational content: a three-step rail, subject context, direct format choices, source status, and the next actionable control. Long explanatory panels were removed from the entry and source workflow.
