# Quiz-Creation Wizard Verification

## Live review — 13 August 2026

The initial Grade 10 Physics teacher screen presents only **Step 1: Choose format**. The source chooser, upload/paste controls, title field, question-count configuration, difficulty configuration, and generation actions are not shown at this stage.

Selecting **MCQ** advances the interface to **Step 2: Choose source material**. The live view then shows two distinct source paths—approved textbook chapters or custom lesson material—together with a clear readiness message and a disabled continuation action until both a valid source and title are provided. The progress rail correctly marks Step 1 complete and Step 2 active.

The review confirms the requested progressive disclosure, reduced visual clutter, and clear forward/back navigation are currently implemented. No additional interface change is required solely to satisfy the wizard request; the existing automated coverage should be retained.
