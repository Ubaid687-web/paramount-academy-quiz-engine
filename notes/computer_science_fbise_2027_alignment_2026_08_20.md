# Grade 10 Computer Science FBISE 2027 Alignment Audit

## Authority and decision rule

The active factual authority is the retained **FBISE Grade 10 Computer Science NCP 2022–23 supplied textbook**. It has eight active reviewed units: Computer Systems; Computational Thinking & Algorithms; Programming Fundamentals; Data and Analysis; Applications of Computer Science; Impacts of Computing; Digital Literacy; and Entrepreneurship in Digital Age. A generated question may test only the selected textbook scope. Model and annual papers supply **format, wording, structure, and cognitive-balance evidence only**.

No paper is treated as a forecast of the 2027 examination. Repetition in past papers is not a reason to force a topic into a selected chapter, and a historical question is never copied, paraphrased, or used as answer evidence.

## Cross-paper findings that may guide live generation

The active model, 2024, and 2025 profiles consistently support a compact Section A register: a single direct question focus; four short, parallel options; and no decorative scenario. Stable forms are direct identification, purpose/function, short code or logic trace, classification, comparison, conversion, and a familiar application only where the selected textbook teaches it.

The reviewed 2026 material independently supports breadth across the current NCP 2022–23 book rather than a narrow programming-only emphasis. This is a **calibration signal, not a prediction**. For a whole-book or multi-unit request, coverage should be breadth-first across the selected units. For a single-unit request, coverage remains breadth-first inside that unit. The generator must not use a past paper to select topics outside the teacher-selected scope.

## Live-generation calibration

The live shared style anchor will add a Grade 10 Computer Science rule set derived from the reviewed active model/2024/2025 profiles. It will require direct `Which`, `What`, `Why`, or `How` wording where natural; concise answer-shaped options; source-supported code/output or logic tracing only in relevant programming/logic content; and different concepts or facets across a draft. It will explicitly prohibit treating 2024/2025 topic recurrence as a 2027 forecast.

The two 2026 records remain visible for Portal Admin review but remain excluded from live pattern routing until explicitly activated through the existing Curriculum Library workflow. This preserves the project rule that no evidence becomes live without Portal Admin control.
