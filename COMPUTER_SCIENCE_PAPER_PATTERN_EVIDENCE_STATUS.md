# Grade 10 Computer Science Paper-Pattern Evidence Status

## Purpose

This document records the current evidence boundary for Grade 10 Computer Science assessment preparation. It deliberately distinguishes **textbook-grounded content preparation** from **paper-pattern claims**. No model-paper layout, past-paper frequency, marks distribution, or examination-format rule is asserted without a recoverable user-supplied paper source.

## Enforced operating rule

The active textbook grounds **what** may be assessed. The three active, extracted, user-supplied papers ground the currently available Computer Science paper structure: **13 compulsory MCQs (13 marks), 11 paired-OR short-response parts (22 marks), and 4 paired-OR extended-response questions (20 marks), totalling 55 marks over 3 hours**. The system uses that structure only when the corresponding supplied paper records are selected.

The approved intake sequence preserved the exact PDF and its OCR transcript in managed storage, recorded the subject, year, type, source provenance, and active status, and then exposed only the supported drafting capability. Any future paper that differs materially will be preserved and reviewed as its own source before changing this framework.
