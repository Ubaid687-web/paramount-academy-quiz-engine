# Grade 10 Computer Science Paper-Pattern Evidence Status

## Purpose

This document records the current evidence boundary for Grade 10 Computer Science assessment preparation. It deliberately distinguishes **textbook-grounded content preparation** from **paper-pattern claims**. No model-paper layout, past-paper frequency, marks distribution, or examination-format rule is asserted without a recoverable user-supplied paper source.

## Current evidence register

| Assessment work | Current source evidence | Permitted platform behavior | Deliberately unavailable behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| MCQ draft | Active Grade 10 Computer Science textbook, curriculum set 60001 | Automatic conceptual MCQ draft generation from selected approved units, with source references and learning objectives | Claiming a model- or past-paper-derived MCQ distribution |
| Short-question draft | Active Grade 10 Computer Science textbook, curriculum set 60001 | Manual, source-grounded short-question drafting with marks and marking guidance | Claiming a past-paper short-question pattern or compiling an exam section from an inferred pattern |
| Long-question draft | Active Grade 10 Computer Science textbook, curriculum set 60001 | Manual, source-grounded long-question drafting with marks and rubric | Claiming a past-paper long-question pattern or expected-choice structure |
| Full-paper draft | No active Computer Science paper-pattern source | No full-paper drafting | Creating, enabling, or representing a full-paper format as FBISE-pattern evidence |

## Enforced operating rule

The active textbook is sufficient to ground **what** may be assessed. It is not sufficient to prove **how an examination paper is structured**. Therefore, the platform keeps the Computer Science full-paper mode unavailable until an original user-supplied model paper or past-paper file is extracted and activated as an `assessmentPatternSources` record.

When a paper is provided, the approved intake sequence is to preserve the exact file in managed storage, extract it, record its subject, year, type, source provenance, and active status, and then expose only the paper-supported drafting capabilities. Until that sequence is completed, no paper-derived marks allocation, optional-question layout, or section pattern may be generated.
