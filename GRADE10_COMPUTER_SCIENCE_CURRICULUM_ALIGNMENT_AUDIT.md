# Grade 10 Computer Science Curriculum Alignment Audit

## Why This Audit Is Required

The active Grade 10 Computer Science source is the supplied **NCP 2022–23 textbook**. The reviewed 2024 and 2025 annual papers contain several older-style question forms, including C-language syntax, loops, switch statements, Boolean logic, and HTML. Therefore, past-paper recurrence alone is not enough to decide what students should memorise for the 2027 examination.

> **Rule:** A paper demand becomes Grade 10 readiness guidance only when the active textbook supports the specific concept. A past-paper topic that is absent, unclear, or only loosely mentioned in the active textbook is marked **unconfirmed** until its exact textbook section or a later official paper confirms it.

## Initial Keyword-Level Audit

| Observed paper-demand family | Active textbook signal | Current status | Safe use now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Algorithms and flowcharts | Found across Computational Thinking & Algorithms and related units | Provisionally aligned | Analyse only after citing the exact textbook subtopic/exercise. |
| C language, loops, and switch statements | No direct active-textbook match for the queried C-language/loop/switch phrases | Unconfirmed | Do not make C-specific readiness claims or generate C-specific practice from older papers alone. |

| Boolean logic, logic gates, Karnaugh-map forms | Broad related terms appear across units, but exact skill scope is not yet established | Needs section-level confirmation | Do not treat Karnaugh or logic-circuit task forms as current readiness requirements without exact textbook support. |
| Data, AI, and machine learning | Strong presence across Data and Analysis, Applications of Computer Science, Digital Literacy, and Entrepreneurship units | Provisionally aligned | Map exact concepts/exercises to paper-style transformations. |
| Digital literacy, cybersecurity, and ethics | Strong presence across later units | Provisionally aligned | Map exact concepts/exercises to paper-style transformations. |

## Consequence for 2027 Preparation

The planned readiness map will not simply repeat 2024/2025 topics. It will first establish which textbook concepts are current, then examine whether the papers test those concepts through direct recall, explanation, comparison, simple scenario, trace, calculation, data/table, or code-like application. The complete 2026 paper is especially important because it can clarify which forms persisted under the current curriculum.

## Evidence Boundaries

This audit does not use the SSC-I Physics methodology reference as Computer Science evidence. It does not use the scanned PBA pages as written-paper evidence. Grade 9 material is excluded entirely.
